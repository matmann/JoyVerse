**Release 1 for testers – alpha.**

Use it online at **https://matmann.github.io/JoyVerse/** – or download **JoyVerse-v{VERSION}-alpha.zip** below, unzip it and open `JoyVerse v{VERSION} alpha.html` in Brave, Chrome or Edge. Everything is in that one file – no install, works offline, and it picks up new devices when you're online. `Read me first.txt` explains the rest.

**In this release**
- Getting started walkthrough: reads your Star Citizen controls export, recognises your devices (incl. vJoy / Joystick Gremlin / Gobloon), modifier and shifted buttons
- Devices: VIRPIL Constellation Alpha L/R, Control Panel #1–#3; VKB Gladiator NXT EVO L/R and EVO Omni Throttle L/R
- Game check, double taps, keyboard keys in the action list, your own map names
- Moving things like the designer, page grows to fit, view-mode legend filter
- Colour schemes (Light, Graphite, Obsidian, Gunmetal, Espresso, Latte, Midnight) and your own
- 🎮 Find: press a joystick button to jump to its label

**Reporting a problem:** a screenshot, your map file (Map file → Save map file) and your controls export.
