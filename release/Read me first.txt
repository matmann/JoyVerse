JOYVERSE – v{VERSION} {STAGE_UPPER} (release 1 for testers)
================================================

A control map maker for Star Citizen: it reads your Star Citizen controls
export and draws every binding on pictures of your sticks and panels, ready
to print or save as a picture.

This is an ALPHA: things will be rough in places. Please tell me what breaks
or confuses you.


GETTING STARTED
---------------
1. Unzip this folder anywhere (Desktop is fine).
2. Open "JoyVerse v{VERSION} {STAGE}.html" in Brave, Chrome or Edge.
   It runs offline in your browser – nothing is installed or sent anywhere.
   When you're online it also picks up new and updated devices.
   (Or skip the download: https://matmann.github.io/JoyVerse/ )
3. The "Getting started" walkthrough opens by itself. Have these ready:
   - Your Star Citizen controls export (the main one). In game:
     Options > Keybindings > Control Profiles > Save Control Settings.
     It's saved in:
     ...\StarCitizen\LIVE\user\client\0\controls\mappings\
   - Optional: your Joystick Gremlin / Gobloon profile (if you use vJoy),
     or your VIRPIL VPC Configurator profiles (for shifted buttons).
4. Follow the steps: it recognises your devices, places your bindings,
   and asks about your modifier / shift button.


SAVE YOUR WORK
--------------
Your map is kept in this browser automatically. To keep a copy or move it
to another PC, use  Map file > Save map file  (and Open map file to bring
it back). Export page as PNG makes a picture for printing or a second screen.

THINGS WORTH TRYING
-------------------
- Getting started with your own controls export.
- Moving things on the map: drag an icon, a heading or a label; click an
  icon for its group's "Move group" tab and line dots. Ctrl+Z undoes.
- Game check (top bar): compares the map with your game file.
- View mode, then click a colour in the legend to show only those actions.
- Colours: the scheme menu in the top bar, or "Edit colours..." to make your own.
- Find (top bar): press a button on your joystick and the map jumps to it.


KNOWN LIMITS
------------
- Browsers can only see buttons 1–32 of a joystick (for Find and the
  joystick button in the label editor).
- With vJoy, every device is called "vJoy Device" in the game file, so the
  walkthrough guesses which is which – check the "Your devices" step.
- Only the devices listed above have pictures so far.


REPORTING A PROBLEM
-------------------
Send me:
1. A screenshot of what looks wrong.
2. Your map file (Map file > Save map file).
3. Your Star Citizen controls export, if it's about bindings.
The version number is at the top left (v{VERSION} {STAGE_UPPER}).
